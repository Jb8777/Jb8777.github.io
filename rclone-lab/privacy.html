Privacy policy for rclone-lab

Last updated: 20 October 2026

rclone-lab is a personal OAuth client used only by its owner, Jamie Buse.

What data is accessed

The app accesses only the owner's own Google Drive data, and only to copy it to the owner's own private backup storage (Backblaze B2) using rclone.

What is not done

  • No data is collected about anyone.
  • No data is shared, sold or sent to anyone other than the owner's own storage.
  • No analytics, tracking or cookies are used.
  • No other user's data is accessed.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
